## Service Descriptions

**Bathroom Renovations** — Turn a tired bathroom into a calm, spa-like retreat.
We design and build complete bathroom renovations — walk-in showers, freestanding
baths, premium tiling, underfloor heating and flawless waterproofing. Every join
sealed, every tile aligned, every detail considered.

**Kitchen Renovations** — The kitchen is the heart of the home, so it deserves to
be done right. As kitchen renovation specialists, we fit bespoke kitchens,
worktops, splashbacks and integrated appliances with precise carpentry and clean,
modern lines.

**Tiling** — Crisp, perfectly aligned tiling makes all the difference. Our expert
tiling contractors work with porcelain, ceramic, natural stone and large-format
tiles across floors, walls and wet areas — sealed, grouted and finished to a
flawless standard.

**Flooring Installation** — Beautiful flooring transforms a room, and we install
it properly. From engineered and solid wood to luxury vinyl tile (LVT), we level
subfloors, lay with precision and finish every edge cleanly. Durable, premium
floors that last.

**Laminate Flooring** — Smart, hard-wearing and superb value. We handle proper
subfloor preparation, acoustic underlay, precise cutting and neat trims, so your
new floor looks seamless and feels solid underfoot for years.

**Home Refurbishment & Building Services** — Planning something bigger? Our
general building and home refurbishment service brings everything under one
trusted team — plastering, partitions, carpentry, decorating and full project
management. One point of contact, one consistent standard.
