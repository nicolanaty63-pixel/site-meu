# Photography Brief — Nicolla Contractors Portfolio

For a photographer (or a capable phone shooter on site). Goal: authentic,
documentary-style images of real projects — natural, premium, believable. Not
staged-perfect, not CGI.

## The look
- **Natural light first.** Shoot near windows; turn off mixed artificial lights
  where possible. Soft daylight, real shadows.
- **Documentary, handheld feel.** Slightly candid angles, real depth of field
  (35–50mm equivalent, f/2.8–f/5.6). Avoid dead-centre symmetry.
- **Keep it real.** A little dust, tools mid-use, protective sheets, offcuts —
  these read as genuine craftsmanship. Don't over-tidy or over-edit.
- **Colour:** natural white balance, no heavy filters, no over-saturation.

## Gear & settings
- DSLR/mirrorless or a recent phone in RAW/ProRAW.
- Tripod **required for before/after pairs** (see below).
- Bracket exposure in bright rooms; expose for the windows, lift shadows gently.

## Shot list (per project)
**Bathroom**
- Wide of the finished room from the doorway.
- Detail: tiling/grout lines, tap or shower fixture, a wet surface with a real
  reflection.
- In-progress: tiles being set, spacers, level on the wall.

**Kitchen**
- Wide of the finished kitchen.
- Detail: worktop edge, splashback, cabinet handles.
- In-progress: a unit being fitted, tools/materials placed naturally.

**Flooring**
- Worker laying a plank (mallet + tapping block in frame), kneeling.
- Detail: plank join, spacers at the skirting, underlay roll.
- Wide of the finished floor with natural light raking across it.

**General / refurbishment**
- One strong "hero" wide per project for the card thumbnail.
- Candid of the team working (great for trust).

## Before / after method (important)
1. Before any work: mount the camera on a tripod, note the **height, position,
   lens and exposure**.
2. Take the "before" shot.
3. After completion, recreate the **exact** framing for the "after".
4. Consistent alignment makes the website's drag-slider look seamless.

## Do / don't
- ✅ Real tools, dust sheets, natural mess, daylight, candid angles.
- ✅ A few imperfections — they sell authenticity.
- ❌ Staged showroom perfection, plastic gloss, HDR halos, heavy vignettes,
  over-sharpening, dead-centre symmetry.

## Deliverables (match the website pipeline)
- 1 hero/card image per project (~4:3) + a before/after pair (~16:9) where
  possible.
- Export **.webp or .jpg**, ~1600px long edge, < 300 KB each.
- Name files per `public/projects/README.md` and drop them in `/public/projects`.
