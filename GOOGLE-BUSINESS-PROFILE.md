# Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization — Nicolla Contractors Ltd

Your Google Business Profile is the single biggest lever for "near me" and
local map rankings. Work through this checklist. Keep NAP (Name, Address,
Phone) **identical** to the website everywhere.

## 1. Claim & verify
- Create/claim the profile at business.google.com.
- Complete verification (video/postcard). Don't skip — unverified profiles don't rank.

## 2. Core business info (must match the website exactly)
- **Name:** Nicolla Contractors Ltd (no keyword stuffing — against Google's rules)
- **Phone:** your real number (replace the website placeholder too)
- **Website:** https://www.nicollacontractors.co.uk
- **Quote link:** point the "appointment/booking" link to `/free-quote`
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 8:00–18:00 (match the website)

## 3. Categories
- **Primary:** General contractor
- **Secondary:** Bathroom remodeler · Kitchen remodeler · Flooring contractor · Tile contractor · Construction company
- The primary category drives the most ranking weight — choose the one matching your highest-value service.

## 4. Service area (since you visit customers)
- Set as a **service-area business** and list: Kings Langley, Watford, Hemel Hempstead, St Albans, Rickmansworth, Berkhamsted, North London.
- These mirror the website's `/areas/*` landing pages — keep them consistent.

## 5. Services
Add each with a short keyword-rich description (mirror the site's service pages):
- Bathroom Renovations · Kitchen Renovations · Tiling · Laminate Flooring · Flooring Installation · Home Refurbishment & Building.

## 6. Business description (max 750 characters)
> Nicolla Contractors Ltd is a premium construction and renovation company based in Kings Langley, Hertfordshire. We specialise in bathroom and kitchen renovations, tiling, laminate and flooring installation, and complete home refurbishment across Hertfordshire and North London. Rated 4.9/5 by 100+ homeowners, we're known for punctual, professional, tidy work and a high-quality finish. Fully insured with a workmanship guarantee. Free, no-obligation quotes.

## 7. Photos & video (add 15+ and refresh monthly)
- Logo + cover photo (branded, charcoal/gold).
- Before/after shots of bathrooms, kitchens, tiling, flooring.
- Team on site (builds trust + authenticity signals).
- Geotag photos where possible; name files descriptively (e.g. `bathroom-renovation-watford.jpg`).

## 8. Reviews (the #1 trust + ranking factor)
- Aim for a steady flow (e.g. 2–4/month) — velocity matters more than a one-off batch.
- Ask every happy customer; send a direct review link by text/email after completion.
- **Respond to every review** (positive and negative) — mention the service + town naturally ("Thanks for the kind words about your Watford bathroom renovation").
- Funnel MyBuilder reviews too; link the website's testimonials to build cross-platform trust.

## 9. Google Posts (weekly)
- Post recent projects, offers, seasonal tips. Each post = a fresh-content signal and a CTA to `/free-quote`.

## 10. Q&A
- Seed 5–8 common questions yourself (areas covered, free quotes, insured, timescales) and answer them — mirrors the website FAQ.

## 11. Attributes
- "Online estimates", "Onsite services", "LGBTQ+ friendly", "Identifies as…" if applicable.

## 12. Citations & NAP consistency (off-GBP local SEO)
- List the identical NAP on: Bing Places, Yell, Checkatrade/MyBuilder, Trustatrader, Houzz, Facebook, Apple Maps.
- Inconsistent NAP across the web dilutes local rankings — audit and fix.

## 13. Tracking
- Add a UTM to the website link: `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp&utm_campaign=profile`.
- Review GBP Insights (calls, direction requests, quote-link clicks) monthly.

bathroom renovation specialists · kitchen renovation company · flooring contractors London ·
laminate flooring installation · tiling services UK · home refurbishment Hertfordshire.
